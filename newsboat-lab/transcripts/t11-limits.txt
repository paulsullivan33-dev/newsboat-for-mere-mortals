# max-items caps how much of a feed Newsboat keeps. First, no cap:

$ newsboat -u setups/limits/urls -c setups/limits/cache.db -x reload
$ sqlite3 setups/limits/cache.db "SELECT count(*) FROM rss_item;"
8

# The same feed, a fresh cache, and a config that says max-items 3:

$ cat setups/limits/config
max-items 3
$ rm setups/limits/cache.db
$ newsboat -u setups/limits/urls -c setups/limits/cache.db -C setups/limits/config -x reload
$ sqlite3 setups/limits/cache.db "SELECT title FROM rss_item ORDER BY pubDate;"
Why seedlings lean toward the window
Compost: the three-bin habit
Watering tomatoes without drowning them

# Only the three newest survive; the five older articles are gone
# from the cache, not just hidden.
