# Two different settings tidy the cache, and they are easy to confuse.
# The lab read the source to be sure, then proved each one.

# A: cleanup-on-quit (default: yes) removes feeds you unsubscribed.
# A copy of the main setup loses the podcast line from its urls:

$ grep -v workshop-podcast urls > cleanhome/urls
$ sqlite3 cache.db "SELECT count(*) FROM rss_item;"   # before
14
# open Newsboat on the copy and quit again:
--- screen: the feed list before quitting ---
   Newsboat 2.34.1 - Your feeds (0 unread, 2 total)
>>    1         (0/8) The Example Garden Notes
      2         (0/4) The Sample Kitchen Journal

$ sqlite3 cache.db "SELECT count(*) FROM rss_item;"   # after
12
$ sqlite3 cache.db "SELECT count(*) FROM rss_feed;"
2
# The two podcast articles and their feed row are really gone.

# B: delete-read-articles-on-quit (default: no) is the read-article
# setting -- and it only flags rows. Another copy, all 14 articles
# already read, gets the setting and one open-and-quit:

$ sqlite3 cache.db "SELECT count(*) FROM rss_item;"            # rows
14
$ sqlite3 cache.db "SELECT count(*) FROM rss_item WHERE deleted = 1;"
14
# Reopening the same setup, the flagged articles are invisible:
--- screen: the feed list before quitting ---
   Newsboat 2.34.1 - Your feeds (0 unread, 3 total)
>>    1         (0/0) The Example Garden Notes
      2         (0/0) The Sample Kitchen Journal
      3         (0/0) The Workshop Bench Podcast

# The rows stay in the file until the feed leaves the urls file
# (cleanup above) -- one reason a cache can look bigger than the
# reading it shows.
